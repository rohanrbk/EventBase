Ask this keynote

Answers grounded in this session
What are the main points?

Anika’s central idea is to connect environmental data to practical neighborhood decisions:

  1. Make invisible problems visible. Local measurements help people understand their own air.
  2. Design for the last mile. Put useful information where decisions already happen.
  3. Build with communities. Trust and participation are part of the system.
